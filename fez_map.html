Methodology: Walking time = haversine distance to nearest medina gate × 1.3 (medina navigation overhead) ÷ 5 km/h. Gate set: Bab Bou Jeloud (W, main tourist entry), Bab Guissa (N), Bab Ftouh (E), Bab R'cif (S). Hotels inside the medina report time to Bab Bou Jeloud specifically.  ·  Hotel Sahrai sits on the Ville Nouvelle hilltop; on-foot to Bab Bou Jeloud is ~25–30 min downhill (steeper return); a cab is ~10–15 min and is the practical default.  ·  Caveats: Interior-medina riad coordinates are derb-level approximations (±50–100m). The medina polygon is the OpenStreetMap administrative boundary for Arrondissement de Fès-Médina (OSM relation 2799583, admin_level=10) — the same dataset Google Maps cross-references for the highlighted historic district. 136 vertices, fetched via Overpass API 2026-06-28.
