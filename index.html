Winning with Mamounia — three moves

1. Specify smoke-free + recently-renovated room category at booking. Two recurring 2024–25 complaints are aging fixtures and surprise smells.

Travel Advisor Notes

What we know that the guidebook doesn't

Heat Strategy

The trip is built around the heat, not against it

Marrakech and Fez run 95–105°F end of August through early September. Day shape: medina + sights in the cool of morning, pool / siesta / hammam mid-day, runs after sunset (Menara Gardens 7 PM, Jnan Sbil 7:30 PM). The Atlas leg breaks the pattern — three days at ~75°F altitude (Tamadot is 1,200m in the Toubkal foothills) with Toubkal-foothill trail running in the actual cool air. This is the variant's whole point.

Friday Rule

Moroccan medinas are dead on Fridays — Jumu'ah closes the souks

In every Moroccan medina, Friday midday prayer (~12:30–1:30 PM) shuts the souks from late morning through Asr (~3–4 PM); many shops stay closed all day. Bab Bou Jeloud and architectural landmarks are still photogenic and accessible — souk wandering and full medina tours are not.

B2R impact: Fri 9/4 lands in Marrakech, not Fez, and we're checked into Mamounia. Friday = property day + Agafay Desert buggy sunset. Zero medina venturing on Friday by design. Marrakech medina moves to Sat 9/5 when souks are alive.

L'Asiatique dress code — enforced, not aspirational

Elegant attire, no shorts after 6 PM, cloakroom for bags

La Mamounia enforces the L'Asiatique dress code at the door: elegant attire, no plastic sandals / flip-flops, no shorts after 6 PM. Backpacks and large bags go to the Ksar Gate cloakroom before dinner. Pack a proper button-down + slacks for Sat 9/5. Same standard applies to Le Rooftop @ Royal Mansour Casablanca Sun 9/6 — assume smart evening attire.

Rick's Café — closed

Annual summer break — verified 2026-07-01

Rick's Café Casablanca closes annually for a summer break that spans our Sun 9/6 window. Le Rooftop @ Royal Mansour Casablanca is the replacement (and the upgrade). La Sqala also considered as a backup but dropped in favor of Le Rooftop.

Volubilis

Half day, not full — and skip Meknes

Volubilis is small. 1.75 hours with a local guide focused on the Orpheus + Bacchus + Diana mosaics, the basilica, the triumphal arch, and the Decumanus Maximus is enough. Adding Meknes proper turns Sat into a slog after the JFK redeye. Moulay Idriss viewpoint on the way in — pretty photo, ~30-min tagine lunch stop. Total day-trip add ~$315–365/2 pax (via Riad Fès private van + Fez-licensed guide).
